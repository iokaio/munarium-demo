# Demo data rights and provenance

The source inventories and per-document hashes are in [manifest.json](manifest.json)
and each pack's `_manifest.json`. Documents are included for demonstration;
answer keys, caches, visitor stores, and operational records are excluded.

| Corpus | Material | Rights and attribution |
|---|---|---|
| Support | Generated fictional multi-system records | Ioka-authored corpus material: Apache-2.0 |
| Data room | Fictional acquisition records and planted inconsistencies | Ioka-authored corpus material: Apache-2.0 |
| Advisory | Fictional household financial records | Ioka-authored corpus material: Apache-2.0 |
| Threat intel | Fictional campaigns, vendors, actors and indicators | Ioka-authored corpus material: Apache-2.0 |
| History | Historical originals, transcriptions, OCR and catalogue metadata from Library of Congress collections | Original source rights retained; source URLs accompany records. Historical originals are public-domain material; do not infer a new license over third-party editorial additions. |
| Patents | USPTO records, derived summaries and generated novelty assessments | Original source rights retained. Ioka-authored generated assessments: Apache-2.0. Generated assessments are not official USPTO opinions. |

Copyright (c) 2026 Ioka LLC applies to Ioka-authored material, not the historical
or government records themselves. Preserve embedded source identifiers and
attribution. Synthetic names, finances, addresses and indicators are fictional
fixtures, not records of customers or visitors. No corpus has been relicensed by
adding the root software LICENSE. U.S. public-domain status need not imply the
same status in every jurisdiction.

Source guidance: [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/legal/) and
[USPTO copyright information](https://www.uspto.gov/terms-use-uspto-websites#copyright).
Some third-party contributions to public collections can retain copyright.
Review the source item's notices when reusing records outside this demonstration.
